TalentFlow AI Design Directions

Six direction sketches for the ATS experience. The chosen direction keeps operational work visible, reduces cognitive load, and gives AI insights a clear but secondary role.

Chosen

Operational Command Center

Best for high-volume recruiting teams that need a clear shell, fast scanning, and detail review without losing context.

  • Persistent navigation shell
  • Board or table with strong filters
  • Sticky candidate detail drawer
  • AI summary and audit in one place

Alt 1

Candidate Story View

Focuses on a narrative profile page where the candidate becomes the center of the experience.

  • Profile-first hierarchy
  • Timeline and notes emphasized
  • Useful for interviewers and managers
  • Less suited to high-volume scanning
Alt 2

Minimal Workflow

Reduces chrome to keep the interface simple, but may underplay the complexity of ATS operations.

  • Fewer panels and fewer controls
  • Lightweight visual language
  • Good for smaller teams
  • Risk: hiding too much context
Alt 3

AI Spotlight

Places scoring and generated insights at the forefront. Strong for differentiation, but it must not overwhelm workflow control.

  • Insight card at the top
  • Scoring and recommendations visible first
  • Works well for review sessions
  • Risk: too much emphasis on model output
AI scoreStrengths
Alt 4

Dense Operations

Optimized for power users who want maximum information density and compact controls.

  • Compact tables and filters
  • Excellent for experts
  • Risk: intimidating for new users
  • Best paired with strong onboarding
Alt 5

Board First

Makes pipeline movement feel immediate and visual, with a Kanban-like workflow at the center.

  • Excellent for stage movement
  • Intuitive for recruiters
  • Needs strong detail affordances
  • Good fit for the core workflow
Recommended direction: Operational Command Center with Guided AI Highlights.
This approach balances scanning, review, and action while keeping the interface calm and trustworthy.
